# ExecutiveOS Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary ICP

**The Executive Office of a mid-market company (200–2,000 employees, $50M–$500M revenue) led by a CEO or COO with a Chief of Staff or EA who manually assembles leadership intelligence.**

### Firmographics

| Attribute | Primary ICP |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Industries** | B2B SaaS, professional services, fintech, healthtech, industrial tech, PE-backed platforms |
| **Company size** | 200–2,000 employees |
| **Revenue** | $50M–$500M ARR/revenue |
| **Geography** | US, UK, Australia initially; English-first |
| **Tech stack** | Microsoft 365 (required for calendar intelligence today), Stripe/modern finance stack |
| **Org structure** | C-suite + 3–8 direct reports; dedicated Chief of Staff or senior EA |

### Executive Personas

| Persona | Title | Role in Deal | Pain |
|---------|-------|--------------|------|
| **Economic Buyer** | CEO, COO | Signs budget | Drowning in inputs; needs clarity on priorities and risks |
| **Champion** | Chief of Staff, Head of Strategy | Drives evaluation | Spends 5–10 hrs/week on briefs, prep, and follow-ups |
| **User** | CFO, CRO, VP Ops | Daily advisor usage | Needs domain-specific intelligence without another dashboard |
| **Technical Influencer** | CIO, CISO | Security/procurement | Cares about SSO, data residency, AI governance |
| **Blocker (sometimes)** | IT who pushes Copilot-only | Standardization | "We already have Microsoft" |

### Typical Problems

1. No single view of initiative health vs. strategic objectives
2. Meeting prep is manual and inconsistent before board/exec sessions
3. Decisions live in email; rationale is lost within 90 days
4. Risks and opportunities are discussed but not tracked
5. Generic AI tools don't know their company, objectives, or team
6. Chief of Staff is a bottleneck for executive information flow

### Success Metrics (What They Care About)

| Metric | Target Outcome |
|--------|----------------|
| **Decision velocity** | Faster resolution of pending decisions |
| **Meeting prep time** | 30–50% reduction for CoS |
| **Strategic alignment** | Initiatives visibly tied to objectives |
| **Executive time reclaimed** | 2–4 hours/week per executive |
| **Risk visibility** | Material risks surfaced before they escalate |
| **Board readiness** | Faster board pack narrative assembly |

### Budget Expectations

| Plan | Expected Buyer | Annual Budget Range |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Professional** | CoS-led team (≤10 seats) | $12K–$18K/year |
| **Executive** | Full exec office (≤25 seats) | $30K–$45K/year |
| **Enterprise** | Division or group | $100K+/year custom |

Primary ICP typically approves **$15K–$40K ACV** without board involvement if security review is light.

### Buying Triggers

- New CEO/COO in first 90 days (operating system reset)
- Board pressure on execution visibility
- Chief of Staff hire (tooling budget unlocked)
- Failed Copilot rollout ("it's just search + chat")
- Post-acquisition integration (multiple leadership teams)
- Calendar overload / burnout signal from CEO
- Strategic planning cycle (Q4 or fiscal year start)

### Buying Objections

| Objection | Root Cause | Response Direction |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|
| "We have Copilot" | Category confusion | Copilot assists documents; ExecutiveOS runs executive operating rhythm |
| "Another AI tool" | Tool fatigue | One platform replaces brief assembly + scattered chats |
| "Security concerns" | AI data handling | RLS, org isolation, no training on customer data (contractual) |
| "Our execs won't adopt" | UX skepticism | Proactive dashboard — value without prompting |
| "Too early" | Beta risk | Controlled beta with white-glove onboarding |
| "Build internally" | CIO pride | 12-month build vs. 2-week time-to-value |

## Secondary ICP

**Enterprise division leaders ($1B+ parent company, 100–500 person P&L) who need an executive intelligence layer independent of corporate IT's Copilot rollout.**

### Firmographics

| Attribute | Secondary ICP |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Industries** | Financial services, pharma, energy, retail (division CEOs) |
| **Company size** | 1,000–10,000 (division: 100–500) |
| **Revenue** | $500M–$5B (division: $100M–$1B) |
| **Buyer** | Division President + Chief of Staff |
| **Deal size** | $40K–$120K ACV (Executive or Enterprise plan) |

### Why Secondary

- Longer procurement cycles (8–16 weeks)
- Higher security bar (SSO, DPA, SOC 2 expected at v1.0+)
- Strong LTV but slower beta velocity

**Beta focus:** 2 of 10 beta slots reserved for secondary ICP to validate enterprise procurement path.

## Anti-ICP (Do Not Pursue in Beta)

- Solo founders with no leadership team (< 20 employees)
- Companies without strategic planning discipline
- Organizations requiring on-premise deployment today
- Buyers seeking project management replacement only
- Teams without Microsoft 365 and unwilling to connect calendar
