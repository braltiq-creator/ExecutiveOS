## Decision quality (DQ)

**Purpose:** Evaluate decisions by process quality, not only by outcome luck.

**Core elements (Spetzler et al.):**

1. Appropriate frame  
2. Creative, doable alternatives  
3. Meaningful, reliable information  
4. Clear values and trade-offs  
5. Sound reasoning  
6. Commitment to action  

**When to use:** Material executive decisions; pack recommendation validation; Reality Lab scoring.

**Strengths:** Separates skill from luck; teaches teams a shared language.  
**Limitations:** Can become checklist theatre if not tied to action.

---

## Executive judgement

Judgement is **pattern recognition under time pressure** (Klein) combined with **slow analysis** when stakes and novelty require it (Kahneman System 2).

Exceptional executives:

- Match mode to context (Cynefin)  
- Make assumptions explicit  
- Seek disconfirming evidence  
- Time-box analysis  

---

## Critical thinking

- Claim → evidence → warrant  
- Steelman opposing views  
- Separate facts, interpretations, and asks  
- Ask “what would change my mind?”

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

Recommendations incomplete without frame, alternatives, information quality, trade-offs, reasoning path, and owner commitment.
