**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Purpose of the role

The CRO owns **revenue system integrity**: demand creation, conversion, retention economics, and commitment quality. Exceptional CROs refuse fake pipeline and channel stuffing — they sell what the enterprise can deliver profitably.

---

## Primary responsibilities

- Revenue strategy across new, expand, renew
- Pipeline quality and forecast credibility
- Pricing, packaging, and deal desk governance
- Channel / partner / dealer performance (where relevant)
- Customer acquisition efficiency and retention handoff
- Market feedback into product and capacity

---

## Mental models

| Model | Use |
|-------|-----|
| **Sell-out > sell-in** | Channel inventory is not demand |
| **Quality of revenue** | Recurring, expandable, collectible beats booked vanity |
| **Commitment quality** | A sale that operations cannot keep is a liability |
| **Funnel physics** | Conversion × capacity × cycle time |
| **Concentration risk** | Logo / region / segment dependency |
| **Price as strategy** | Discounting is a decision, not a habit |

|-----------|---------------------|

| Bookings vs deliverability | Deliverability wins |

## Questions · Signals · Indicators

**Questions:** Is this real demand? What is sell-out doing? Can we deliver? What did we discount away? Which segment deserves scarce capacity?

**Signals:** Pipeline coverage & age, win/loss, discount leakage, churn risk, dealer DIO, stage hygiene  

**Leading:** Qualified pipeline creation, sales cycle, engagement depth, early churn flags  

**Lagging:** Revenue, NRR/GRR, CAC payback, lost sales, forecast accuracy  

---

## Rhythms · Success · Warnings

**Rhythms:** Weekly pipeline · Weekly forecast · Monthly demand consensus · Quarterly go-to-market  

**Success:** Bias in band; strategic win rate; healthy retention; clean handoffs to delivery  

**Warnings:** Sandbag/heroic swings; aging pipeline; chronic discounting; channel inventory spike; capacity promises without COO

## Disagreements · Behaviours · Board · Style

**Disagreements:** COO (capacity); CFO (margin/cash); CCO (retention ownership); CSO (segment focus); CEO (growth narrative)

*The Sales Acceleration Formula*; *Predictable Revenue* (critically); Challenger Sale; MEDDICC; Price Intelligently / Monetizing Innovation (pricing).

**Forecast checklist:** stage exit criteria · next step dated · economic buyer · delivery constraint noted · discount justified  

---

## Lessons · Anti-patterns · Evolution · Trends

**Lessons:** You cannot outrun a broken product or capacity with incentives.  

**Anti-patterns:** Sandbag culture; stuffing; discount as default; vanity coverage ratios  

**Evolution:** From head of sales → owner of revenue system (acquire–expand–retain)  

**Trends:** Usage-based pricing; PLG + sales assist; AI forecasting assist; ethical selling scrutiny  

---

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

CRO knowledge must encode commitment quality and sell-out truth — especially in manufacturing dealers and subscription retention.
