# ExecutiveOS Logo System

## Logo Design Criteria

ExecutiveOS marks must communicate: **judgment, clarity, authority, and continuity** — not artificial intelligence.

### Mandatory Avoidances
- Brains, neural networks, robot heads
- AI sparkles, magic wands, stars
- Circuit boards, chip icons
- Generic hexagons, generic nodes
- Gradients that read "startup AI"

### Evaluation Criteria
- Timeless in 10 years
- Legible at 16px (favicon)
- Works in monochrome
- Appropriate in board presentation
- Distinct from Copilot, Notion, Linear

---

## Concept 1 — The Meridian

**Mark:** A single horizontal line with one precise vertical intersection — the moment of decision on a timeline.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Meaning** | The meridian line divides what was from what will be; the intersection is *now* — where judgment happens |
| **Symbolism** | Navigation, precision, the executive's responsibility to decide at the right moment |
| **Scalability** | Excellent — two strokes survive at 16px |
| **App icon** | Strong — centered mark on Ink 900 square |
| **Website** | Strong — pairs with wordmark left |
| **Presentation** | Excellent — subtle, board-appropriate |
| **Merchandise** | Excellent — embossing, enamel pin |

## Recommended Direction

### Primary: **Concept 1 — The Meridian** + Wordmark

**Rationale:**
- Most timeless — survives trend cycles
- Directly encodes "decision at the right moment" (core product value)
- Scales perfectly; works monochrome
- Distinct from AI-product visual language
- Pairs naturally with Intelligence Center priority strip (visual rhyme)

### Secondary lockup: **Concept 5 — The Ledger** for document/export contexts

Use Ledger mark on PDF exports, board packs, and executive report headers where "record of decisions" narrative strengthens the moment.

## Logo Construction Specifications

### Clear Space
Minimum clear space = height of the meridian vertical stroke on all sides.

### Minimum Sizes
| Context | Minimum |
|---------|---------|
| Favicon | 16×16px (mark only) |
| App icon | 32×32px |
| Wordmark | 120px width |
| Presentation title | 180px width |

### Color Variants

| Variant | Mark | Wordmark | Background |
|---------|------|----------|------------|
| Primary | Ink 900 | Ink 900 | White |
| Reversed | White | White | Ink 900 |
| Monochrome | Black | Black | White |
| Monochrome reversed | White | White | Black |

### Incorrect Usage
- Do not rotate the mark
- Do not apply gradients to the mark
- Do not outline the mark
- Do not place on busy photography without scrim
- Do not use sparkles, glow, or animation on the logo
