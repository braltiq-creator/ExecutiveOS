## Systems thinking

**Purpose:** See feedback loops, delays, and unintended consequences.

**Core ideas (Senge et al.):**

- Today’s problems come from yesterday’s solutions  
- Cause and effect are distant in time/space  
- Local optima can destroy system performance  

**Executive use:** Inventory, overtime, discounting, and channel stuffing are classic systems traps.

---

## Complexity (Cynefin — Snowden)

| Domain | Decision posture |
|--------|------------------|
| Clear | Best practice / standard work |
| Complicated | Expert analysis |
| Complex | Probe–sense–respond; safe-to-fail experiments |
| Chaotic | Act–sense–respond; stabilise first |

**Anti-pattern:** Applying complicated analysis to complex domains (false precision).

---

## Scenario planning

**Purpose:** Prepare for multiple plausible futures rather than a single forecast.

**When to use:** Capex, transformation, commodity/geopolitical uncertainty, capacity expansion.

**Strengths:** Improves readiness and strategic conversation.  
**Limitations:** Theatre if scenarios don’t change decisions or budgets.

**Practice:** 3–4 scenarios; signposts; pre-committed moves; owners.

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

Operating loop and Focus Outcomes are systems objects. Reality Lab and futures engines exercise scenario readiness. Packs must not pretend linear KPI trees capture complex industries.
