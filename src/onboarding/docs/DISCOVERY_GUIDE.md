# Executive Discovery Guide

## For executives

1. Sign in and open onboarding.
2. Answer only: role, primary objective, briefing time (industry/timezone only if needed).
3. Connect Microsoft 365 and your operational platform when prompted.
4. Confirm, edit, or ignore a short list of discoveries.
5. Open your first Executive Briefing on Today.

Target: **under 15 minutes**.

## For administrators

- Ensure provider licenses (`provider-microsoft365`, `provider-simpro`) are active.
- Secrets remain in Key Vault — discovery never stores plaintext credentials.
- Monitor Learning banner maturity on Today during the first 30 days.

## Success metrics

Tracked in `measureOnboarding`:

- Time to first Executive Briefing
- Discovery accuracy (confirm/edit vs ignore)
- Manual configuration minutes
- Organisation coverage
- Knowledge Graph completeness
- Confidence growth
- Setup completion rate
