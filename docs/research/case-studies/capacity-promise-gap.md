# CS-02 — Capacity–Promise Gap

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Context

Commercial teams sell timelines or volumes that operations cannot sustainably deliver. Bridged temporarily by overtime, expedites, quality debt, or silent date slips.

---

## What happened *(pattern)*

- Bookings celebrated  
- Capacity envelope exceeded  
- Overtime/contractors become structural  
- Quality or SLA breaches emerge  
- Customers lose trust; margin collapses  

Appears in manufacturing slots, field-service SLAs, professional services overcommit, and SaaS reliability promises.

---

## Executive failure mode

- CRO and COO not forced into shared truth  
- Overtime treated as capacity  
- No scarcity allocation policy  
- Optimism in customer communication  

---

## What exceptional leaders do differently

- Publish demonstrated capacity  
- Allocate scarcity with rules  
- Re-promise early with owners  
- Expire overtime exceptions  

---

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

Commitment quality as CRO+COO object; overtime≠capacity rule; allocation decisions in manufacturing blueprint.
