# Executive Futures Engine

Deterministic **scenario reasoning** from the Digital Twin and Executive Intelligence evidence.

Not machine-learning forecasting. Not a bind.

## What executives get

- What is likely to happen next
- Which assumptions drive that future
- Which decisions can influence the outcome
- Which early warning signals to monitor

## Cases generated

Best Case · Expected Case · Worst Case · Most Likely · Black Swan

Each future explains why it exists, supporting and weakening evidence, interventions, and leading indicators.

## Council

Every council member reviews every future independently. Disagreement is preserved (e.g. CFO cash risk vs COO operational offset vs CSO investment despite pressure).

## Reality Lab

`simulateFuturesForScenario` scores prediction quality, confidence calibration, intervention effectiveness, and decision quality.
