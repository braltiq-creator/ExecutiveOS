# 07 — Executive Rhythms & Meeting Packs

**EIPF:** `meetingPacks()` (+ Reality Lab rhythm awareness)  
**Rule:** Cadence exists to force judgement — not to generate slide theatre.

---

## Operating cadence

| Cadence | Ritual | Owner | Purpose |
|---------|--------|-------|---------|
| **Daily** | Command Centre / plant pulse | CEO + COO | Pulse, overnight shocks, same-day judgements |
| **Daily** | Council observation scan | All roles | Proactive agency on material signals |
| **Weekly** | ELT | CEO | Cross-functional trade-offs; scarce slot calls |
| **Weekly** | Ops review | COO | Schedule, utilisation, supply, overtime |
| **Weekly** | Demand / dealer review | CRO | Forecast, allocation, dealer health |
| **Weekly** | Cash & inventory huddle | CFO | CCC, E&O, clearance economics |
| **Monthly** | Margin & mix committee | CFO + CSO | Contribution, discounting, strategy mix |
| **Monthly** | Operating review | CEO | Outcome portfolio health |
| **Monthly** | Strategy review | CSO | Share, electrification, automation |
| **Quarterly** | Board | CEO / CFO / CSO | Disclosure, capital, risk narrative |
| **Event** | Disruption huddle | COO lead | Supplier/line/safety/logistics shocks |
| **Event** | Stage gate | CSO / CFO | Automation / electrification capital |

## Meeting pack templates

### Pack A — Weekly ELT (`mfg-meet-weekly-elt`)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Cadence** | weekly |
| **Purpose** | Align capacity, demand, cash, and strategy on one judgement surface |
| **Prep minutes** | 25 |
| **Required outcomes** | forecast, utilisation, inventory, dealer, working capital |
| **Discussion sequence** | 1) Pulse & Organisation Health 2) Council observations 3) Scarcity / allocation 4) Inventory & cash 5) Priority decisions 6) Actions & owners |
| **Council focus** | CEO arbitrate · COO capacity truth · CRO demand truth · CFO cash · CSO mix/strategy |

---

### Pack B — Weekly Ops (`mfg-meet-weekly-ops`)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Cadence** | weekly |
| **Purpose** | Stabilize factories and supply for the frozen window |
| **Prep minutes** | 20 |
| **Required outcomes** | utilisation, lead-time, supply-resilience, margin |
| **Discussion sequence** | Constraints → Schedule commit → Overtime exceptions → Supplier risks → Recovery actions |
| **Council focus** | COO lead · CFO cost · CRO promise impact |

---

### Pack C — Weekly Demand Consensus (`mfg-meet-demand`)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Cadence** | weekly |
| **Purpose** | Agree a single demand truth before schedule and inventory move |
| **Prep minutes** | 20 |
| **Required outcomes** | forecast, dealer, share |
| **Discussion sequence** | Sell-out vs sell-in → Regional bias → Construction/commodity signals → Reset decision → Allocation implications |
| **Council focus** | CRO lead · COO feasibility · CFO cash consequence · CSO market narrative |

---

### Pack D — Monthly Margin & Mix (`mfg-meet-margin-mix`)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Cadence** | monthly |
| **Purpose** | Protect contribution while advancing strategy mix |
| **Prep minutes** | 30 |
| **Required outcomes** | margin, share, future-fit, inventory |
| **Discussion sequence** | Margin bridge → Discount/clearance → Strategy mix gap → Capex implications → Decisions |
| **Council focus** | CFO + CSO co-lead · CRO commercial · COO capacity |

---

### Pack E — Quarterly Board Manufacturing (`mfg-meet-board`)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Cadence** | quarterly |
| **Purpose** | Honest board narrative on capacity, inventory, transformation, and risk |
| **Prep minutes** | 90 |
| **Required outcomes** | working capital, supply-resilience, future-fit, share |
| **Discussion sequence** | Enterprise pulse → Outcome portfolio → Material risks → Capital gates → Outlook range |
| **Council focus** | CEO/CSO narrative · CFO numbers · COO operational facts · CRO market |

---

### Pack F — Disruption Huddle (`mfg-meet-disruption`)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Cadence** | event |
| **Purpose** | Contain supplier/line/safety/logistics shocks with owners and time boxes |
| **Prep minutes** | 10 |
| **Required outcomes** | supply-resilience, utilisation, lead-time |
| **Discussion sequence** | Fact pattern → Customer/dealer impact → Options → Decision → Comms |
| **Council focus** | COO lead · others as impact requires |

---

## Rhythm anti-patterns (reject in pack design)

- Separate “factory meeting” and “commercial meeting” that never meet  
- Board packs that omit inventory range  
- Demand meetings that cannot change the schedule  
- Ops meetings that approve overtime without CFO visibility
