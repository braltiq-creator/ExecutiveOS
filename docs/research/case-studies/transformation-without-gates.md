# CS-03 — Transformation Without Gates

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Context

Large digital, automation, or operating-model programmes continue spending after milestones slip, benefits stay unproven, and operational readiness is weak.

---

## What happened *(pattern)*

- Vision slides outrun evidence  
- Stage gates become rubber stamps  
- Scope expands to justify spend  
- Core reliability or culture degrades  
- Programme eventually rebaselined or written down  

---

## Executive failure mode

- Sunken cost defence  
- No kill criteria  
- CFO overruled without residual risk owner  
- Strategy narrative detached from resources  

---

## What exceptional leaders do differently

- Fail closed on missing evidence  
- Separate learning pilots from scale funding  
- Track benefits owners  
- Tell the board a ranged story early  

---

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

Stage-gate frameworks; CSO/CFO council knowledge; Reality Lab transformation scenarios; learning rule on gate-miss-with-spend.
