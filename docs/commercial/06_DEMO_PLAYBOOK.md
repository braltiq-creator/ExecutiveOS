# 06 — Demo Playbook

**Version:** 1.0  
**Sources:** Positioning Bible · Manufacturing blueprint · Product Experience (Command Centre, Council, Profiles, Portal)  
**Rule:** Demo judgement altitude — never a connector catalogue.

---

## Demo principles

1. **Start where the executive starts** — Command Centre / Intelligence Center / Today Brief.  
2. **One Profile per demo** — Operations, Commercial, or Manufacturing.  
3. **Outcomes before systems** — Name three outcomes early; connectors are optional colour.  
4. **Council as judgement, not chat theatre** — Role-consistent perspectives; human decides.  
5. **Leave time for silence** — Executives buy when they recognise their morning.  
6. **Never invent features** — Label roadmap items as roadmap.  

**Length:** Standard 45 minutes · Executive abbreviated 25 minutes · Enterprise deep-dive 60 minutes.

---

## Standard demonstration (45 minutes)

| Min | Section | Surface | Talk track |
|-----|---------|---------|------------|
| 0–3 | Hook | Command Centre | “This is before email — what changed overnight, ranked against outcomes.” |
| 3–10 | Pulse | Brief / Priority strip | Attention, stakes, why it matters — not a KPI wall. |
| 10–18 | Strategy & outcomes | Outcomes / Initiatives | “Strategy is tracked execution with health — not a deck.” |
| 18–25 | Decision | Decision workspace | Pending decisions, trade-offs, recommendation meta — authority stays human. |
| 25–32 | Council | Advisors / Council | Role-consistent judgement for the selected Profile. |
| 32–38 | Knowledge | Graph / Knowledge | Relationships across meetings, decisions, risks — as available in product. |
| 38–42 | Trust & control | Organisation Portal (glance) | Subscription, security posture, systems — customer in control. |
| 42–45 | Close | — | Trial on this Profile, or Design Partner path. |

### Demo don’ts

- Don’t open with chat  
- Don’t apologise for mock or design-partner data — frame as Reality Lab / representative enterprise  
- Don’t feature-dump every integration  
- Don’t claim autonomous executive replacement

## Operations Executive demo

**Buyer:** COO, VP Operations, Head of Delivery  
**Promise:** Capacity, delivery, and operational resilience ranked for judgement.

### Narrative arc

1. Morning Command Centre — delivery risk and capacity tension before stand-ups.  
2. Outcomes — e.g. on-time delivery, utilisation balance, operational resilience.  
3. Decisions — resource allocation, priority conflicts, recovery choices.  
4. Council — COO-weighted operational lens; CEO/CFO stakes where relevant.  
5. Systems (optional, late) — M365 calendar; Simpro or ops systems as context, not the product.  

### Talking points

- “Operations leaders don’t need another status meeting — they need ranked consequence.”  
- “We don’t replace your ops stack; we prepare judgement above it.”  
- “Individual Executive proves the rhythm; Team shares the picture with leadership.”  

### Questions to ask live

- What breaks first when demand spikes — people, process, or partners?  
- Which decision did you re-debate last month because context was scattered?  

### Objections

| Objection | Handle |
|-----------|--------|
| “We have project tools.” | Those track work. This ranks executive attention. |
| “Show me the connector.” | Connect after outcomes — value is judgement, not sync theatre. |

### Value story

> A COO starts the day knowing which delivery commitments are at risk against quarterly outcomes — before the first escalation email.

## Commercial Executive demo

**Buyer:** CRO, CFO (commercial), VP Sales / Revenue  
**Promise:** Pipeline truth, commitment quality, commercial confidence.

### Narrative arc

1. Command Centre — revenue and commitment signals that require judgement today.  
2. Outcomes — e.g. pipeline integrity, win quality, forecast confidence.  
3. Decisions — discount authority, focus accounts, resource against territory.  
4. Council — CRO/CFO tension made explicit; trade-offs visible.  
5. Systems (optional) — Salesforce / CRM as evidence, not the SKU.  

### Talking points

- “Commercial fog is expensive. Clarity is a leadership asset.”  
- “Dashboards show charts. ExecutiveOS prepares the decision.”  
- “You buy Commercial Intelligence — not another CRM seat.”  

### Questions

- Where does forecast confidence actually live today — CRM, spreadsheet, or someone’s head?  
- What would change if the CEO and CRO shared one overnight commercial picture?  

### Objections

| Objection | Handle |
|-----------|--------|
| “Salesforce already does this.” | Salesforce is system of record for revenue activity. We are judgement infrastructure for leadership. |
| “Our reps won’t use it.” | They don’t have to. This is for the people who run the commercial system. |

### Value story

> A CRO walks into the forecast meeting already knowing which commitments deserve challenge — and why.

## Manufacturing Executive demo

**Buyer:** Manufacturing / industrial OEM ELT (CEO, COO, CFO, plant or BU lead)  
**Promise:** Demand, factory, inventory, dealers, working capital — one judgement surface.  
**Product basis:** Manufacturing Executive Intelligence Pack (`pack-manufacturing-executive`) on EIPF; Core unchanged.  
**Docs:** [`../intelligence-packs/manufacturing/`](../intelligence-packs/manufacturing/)

### Narrative arc

1. Command Centre — overnight manufacturing attention (allocation, inventory, demand tension).  
2. Outcomes — factory, inventory, dealer demand, capital as Focus Outcomes (from blueprint).  
3. Decisions — build-slot allocation, inventory posture, dealer priority (catalogue from Manufacturing docs).  
4. Council — manufacturing-aware role knowledge; same CEO/CFO/COO/CRO roles.  
5. Reality Lab (mention) — reasoning validated in simulation before customer exposure.  
6. Systems (optional) — Dynamics / ERP / dealer context as evidence layers — never “we replace ERP.”  

### Talking points

- “Manufacturing executives see factory, inventory, demand, and capital as one problem — software usually splits them.”  
- “The pack brings industry context. The Core stays the Executive Intelligence Platform.”  
- “Dealer demand is not B2B SaaS pipeline — language stays manufacturing-native.”  

### Questions

- Where do build slots and working capital collide in your operating rhythm today?  
- Who owns the morning picture across plants and dealers — and how long does it take to assemble?  

### Objections

| Objection | Handle |
|-----------|--------|
| “We need SAP customisation.” | We sit above systems of record. Packs add industry judgement context — not a MES rewrite. |
| “Prove industry fit.” | Manufacturing blueprint + production pack + Reality Lab validation path. |

### Value story

> An industrial leadership team decides where scarce build slots and cash go — before the quarter breaks — without a custom platform project.

## Hitachi demonstration (enterprise industrial)

**Purpose:** Enterprise-grade demonstration for a Hitachi-class industrial / OEM manufacturing prospect (multi-factory, dealer or distributor channel, material working-capital exposure).  
**Note:** There is no Hitachi-specific product fork in the repository. This is a **commercial demonstration scenario** using the Manufacturing Executive Profile and pack — representative data and Reality Lab patterns, not a custom build.

### Audience

- Economic buyer: Division / company CEO or COO  
- Champion: Chief of Staff, Head of Strategy, or BU transformation lead  
- Influencers: CFO, CIO/CISO, manufacturing systems owners  

### Recommended length

60 minutes (includes security / commercial close).

### Flow

| Min | Focus |
|-----|--------|
| 0–5 | Executive problem: fragmented factory · inventory · demand · capital view |
| 5–20 | Manufacturing Command Centre + overnight ranking (representative enterprise simulation) |
| 20–35 | Decision deep-dive (allocation / inventory / dealer priority) + Council |
| 35–45 | Trust: pack-on-EIPF (Core unchanged), isolation, Portal, no implementation fee trial path |
| 45–55 | Commercial: Enterprise custom vs Team land; Design Partner option |
| 55–60 | Ask: pilot scope + success metrics |

### Executive talking points (Hitachi-class)

1. “You already paid for systems of record. The missing layer is executive judgement continuity.”  
2. “We will not customise Core for manufacturing. The Manufacturing Intelligence Pack is how industry context arrives.”  
3. “Success is not another dashboard — it is a morning that declares what requires leadership attention.”  
4. “Pilot measures habit and decision clarity — not connector count.”  
5. “Authority remains with your executives. The platform prepares; it does not pretend to decide.”  

### Success metrics for a pilot (agree in writing)

- Weekly active executives (WAEx) on Command Centre  
- Decisions logged with rationale  
- Time-to-first useful brief after Discovery  
- Qualitative: “Would you start here before email?”  

### Conversion path

Design Partner Program → Production (Team or Enterprise) per [`08_DESIGN_PARTNER_PROGRAM.md`](./08_DESIGN_PARTNER_PROGRAM.md).
