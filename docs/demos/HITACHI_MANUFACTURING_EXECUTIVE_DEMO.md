# Hitachi Manufacturing Executive Demonstration

**Phase:** 52A  
**Audience:** Live customer / design-partner meeting (Hitachi-class industrial OEM)  
**Duration:** ~60 minutes  
**Product basis:** ExecutiveOS + Manufacturing Executive Intelligence Pack + Domain Advisors  
**Rule:** No custom Core. Permanent Council unchanged. Advisors are specialists, not executives.

---

## Meeting framing (2 minutes)

**Opening (Braltiq):**

> Thank you for the time. Today is not a feature tour. It is a working session on how ExecutiveOS would support the judgements your leadership already makes — national and regional demand, factory ordering, dealer allocation, inventory, and working capital — without replacing Dynamics, dealers, or your plants.
>
> Three rules for the next hour:
> 1. The Executive Council stays CEO, CFO, COO, CRO, CSO — we do not invent new executives.  
> 2. Specialist Domain Advisors — Demand Planning, Inventory, Dealer Network, Production Planning, and others — advise that Council with evidence and challenge.  
> 3. You decide. Always.

**Success for this meeting:** Hitachi leaders recognise their business in the language and the morning rhythm.

---

## Scene setting — representative enterprise (3 minutes)

We use a **representative heavy-equipment OEM** pattern (multi-factory, dealer/branch network, model and variant complexity, material working capital). Numbers below are demonstration-realistic, not Hitachi confidential data.

| Surface | Demo state |
|---------|------------|
| Factories | 3 plants · finite build slots |
| Channel | National → region → branch / dealer |
| Portfolio | Core models + long-tail variants |
| Tension | Order bank vs slots vs inventory vs cash |

## Act 1 — Manufacturing Command Centre (10 minutes)

**Screen:** Morning Executive Brief / Command Centre

**Talk track:**

> This is before email. Overnight, three things moved against your outcomes: regional demand for Model H shifted up in two states; Plant 2 load crossed demonstrated capacity on the frozen horizon; finished-goods days of inventory on Variant H-XL aged past policy.

**Walk the panels (concept — see mockups):**

1. **Organisation Health** — enterprise pulse, not a KPI wall  
2. **Factory Health** — Plant 2 constrained  
3. **Dealer Demand** — branch heat where fill will break  
4. **Production Capacity** — slots vs order bank  
5. **Inventory** — service vs capital  
6. **Working Capital** — cash trapped in ageing mix  
7. **Executive Value** — why this morning matters  
8. **Priority Decisions** — ranked for judgement  

**Question to Hitachi:**

> Which of these would your leadership team argue about first on a Monday — and who owns the argument today?

**Expected recognition:** Factory vs dealer vs capital is one problem, usually split across systems.

---

## Act 2 — Domain Advisors before Council (12 minutes)

**Screen:** Advisor contribution strip (not Council seats)

**Talk track:**

> Before the Council speaks, specialists prepare. They do not vote as executives.

| Advisor | Contribution this morning |
|---------|---------------------------|
| **Demand Planning** | National plan is coherent; **branch** demand for Model H diverges — confidence band widened on variants |
| **Production Planning** | Plant 2 cannot absorb the regional uplift without deferrals or overtime |
| **Inventory** | Rebalancing FG toward constrained branches protects service; ageing H-XL is capital, not safety stock |
| **Dealer Network** | Allocation without published rationale will feel like favouritism in Region West |
| **Manufacturing Advisor** | Enterprise frame: this is a **slot + capital** decision, not a local plant scheduling ticket |

**Challenge examples (spoken):**

- Demand Planning → CRO: “Is Region West branch demand committed, or aspiration?”  
- Production Planning → COO: “Is the plan finite-capacity true on the frozen horizon?”  
- Inventory → CFO: “How much cash is trapped if we protect turns instead of strategic fill?”  

**Line:**

> Advisors challenge assumptions. The Council still forms the enterprise recommendation. You still decide.

---

## Act 3 — Executive Council (10 minutes)

**Screen:** Council panel — CEO · CFO · COO · CRO · CSO

| Role | Stance (demo) |
|------|----------------|
| **CRO** | Protect Region West fill — strategic dealers at risk |
| **COO** | Do not break Plant 2 frozen horizon without overtime authorisation |
| **CFO** | Overtime and FG reposition have capital cost; ageing H-XL should fund part of the move |
| **CSO** | Do not starve strategic Model H for long-tail variants |
| **CEO** | Need one enterprise call: service in West without hiding capital cost |

**Consensus / confidence (demo):**

- **Recommendation posture:** Re-sequence slots toward Model H for West; overtime limited and costed; begin H-XL liquidation; publish allocation rationale to dealers.  
- **Confidence:** Moderate — branch signal fresh; supplier risk not fully closed (Supply Chain Advisor flag).  
- **Dissent preserved:** COO notes overtime fatigue risk; logged, not buried.

## Act 4 — Demand Planning Workspace (8 minutes)

**Walkthrough:**

1. **Demand Heat Map** — national calm; two regions hot  
2. **Model Demand** — Model H uplift  
3. **Variant Demand** — H-XL soft; H-Pro firm  
4. **Dealer Trends** — West branches pulling; gaming check clean  
5. **Forecast Confidence** — hierarchy: national high · variant medium  
6. **Factory Capacity** — Plant 2 binding constraint  
7. **Executive Recommendation** — adopt hierarchical plan with confidence bands; do not national-average away the West signal  

**Hitachi question:**

> Where does your national forecast currently get challenged by branch truth — and how long does that take?

## Act 5 — Factory Ordering Workspace (10 minutes)

**Decision under review:** Recommended factory orders for the next frozen window.

| Block | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Recommended Orders** | Increase Model H slots at Plant 2 (+ overtime option B); defer two long-tail variants |
| **Reasoning** | Outcome-ranked slots; finite capacity; dealer service in West |
| **Council Discussion** | Summary of Act 3 |
| **Advisor Contributions** | Demand · Production · Inventory · Dealer · Supply Chain |
| **Expected Business Outcomes** | Protect West fill; reduce ageing capital; preserve strategic mix |
| **Financial Impact** | Overtime $X; capital release from H-XL $Y; revenue timing protection |
| **Risk** | Supplier A-part lead time (Supply Chain); dealer communication if deferrals opaque |
| **Confidence** | Moderate → rises if Supply Chain clears A-part |

**Close the act:**

> This is judgement infrastructure — not a new MRP. Dynamics remains the system of record. ExecutiveOS prepares the decision.
