## Industry structure

People-leveraged firms: consulting, legal, accounting, engineering services, agencies. Economics driven by utilisation, realisation, and leverage. Reputation and talent are the product.

---

## Value chain

```
Market → Sell → Staff → Deliver → Quality review → Invoice/Collect → Relationship → Expand/Refer
```

---

## Economic drivers

- Utilisation and realisation  
- Leverage model (junior/senior mix)  
- Win rate and rate card integrity  
- WIP and lock-up (DSO)  
- Talent attraction/retention  
- Matter/project margin
