## E.4 — Colour Psychology Deep Dive

### Why navy beats black for ExecutiveOS

Black (#000000) signals: luxury fashion (Chanel), developer tools (Linear), death/formality (varies by culture).  
Navy (#1A1F2E) signals: institution (McKinsey, Goldman), trust (banking), permanence (universities), authority without severity.

ExecutiveOS is not a fashion brand or developer tool. Navy is correct institutional signal.

### Why FT Pink works for morning

#FFF1E5 (salmon pink) signals: Financial Times (editorial authority), morning (warm light), print (paper), calm (not alert red/yellow).  
It is the only colour in the 12-palette exploration that triggers **reading behavior** rather than **software behavior**.

Morning brief is a reading experience, not a dashboard experience. The colour should trigger reading.

### Why critical red must be muted (#9B2226 not #DC2626)

Bright red (#DC2626) triggers amygdala response — fight/flight. Executives in fight/flight make poor decisions.  
Muted red (#9B2226) signals importance without panic. Bloomberg uses orange for similar reason — attention without alarm.

## E.5 — Typography Micro-Study: 15px Body at 8 Hours

Simulated reading comfort evaluation (studio heuristic, not clinical):

| Typeface | Eye fatigue (8hr) | Scan speed | Authority | Recommendation |
|----------|-------------------|------------|-----------|----------------|
| Inter 15px | Medium fatigue | Fast | Low | Reject |
| Geist 15px | Medium fatigue | Fast | Medium | Beta OK |
| IBM Plex 15px | **Low fatigue** | Medium | **High** | **Adopt** |
| Söhne 15px | Low fatigue | Medium | High | Marketing only (license cost for body) |
| SF Pro 15px | Low fatigue | Fast | Medium | iOS only |

**Open counter theory:** IBM Plex's larger open counters reduce fixation strain during long reading sessions (advisor reasoning panels, decision context). Geist's tighter geometry optimizes for code display, not prose reading.

## E.6 — Intelligence Center: Five Layouts Not Pursued (And Why)

### The River
Continuous vertical scroll of all intelligence as a single narrative stream.  
**Rejected:** No hierarchy. Executives need priority order, not chronological flow.

### The Matrix
2×2 grid of equal-weight domains (Strategy, Operations, People, Finance).  
**Rejected:** Equal weight contradicts prioritization thesis. Everything cannot be equally important.

### The Spotlight
Single item fullscreen. Swipe for next. Stories format.  
**Rejected:** Instagram/TikTok pattern. Wrong audience. Hides overview.

### The Terminal
Full Bloomberg mimic — command line primary input, panels secondary.  
**Rejected:** Training requirement. Excludes non-technical executives.

### The Assistant
Chat-first. "Good morning! Here's what I found..."  
**Rejected:** ChatGPT pattern. Reactive not proactive. AI cliché.

## E.8 — Notification Philosophy (Extended)

### The Notification Hierarchy

| Tier | Channel | Frequency cap | Example |
|------|---------|---------------|---------|
| Critical | Push + in-app | 1/day max | "Initiative Aurora off-track. Board exposure." |
| Important | In-app only | 3/day max | "Decision pending >48 hours." |
| Informational | Digest only | Unlimited in digest | "Strategy advisor updated recommendation." |
| Silent | None | — | Health score changed 2 points |

### The Notification Test

Before sending any notification, ask:
1. Would a excellent Chief of Staff interrupt the CEO with this? If no → don't send.
2. Can this wait until morning brief? If yes → defer.
3. Is there a specific action required? If no → digest only.
4. Is this critical to today's board meeting? If yes → send.

**Target metric:** 80% of executive-days with zero push notifications.

## E.14 — The "Brief Me" Verb Strategy

Goal: "Brief me" becomes executive vernacular for checking ExecutiveOS.

### Seeding strategy

| Channel | Tactic |
|---------|--------|
| Product | Voice command "Brief me" in Priority Whisper |
| Marketing | "Brief me." as two-word billboard |
| Beta executives | Encourage "I got briefed this morning" language |
| Conference | Event named "The Briefing" not "ExecutiveOS Summit" |
| Content | Article series: "How I Brief" by CEOs |
| Merchandise | Notebook cover: "Brief." single word |

### Competitive moat

When "brief me" is associated with ExecutiveOS:
- Copilot cannot claim it (reactive, not brief)
- ChatGPT cannot claim it (no organizational context)
- Notion cannot claim it (no proactive intelligence)
- Bloomberg cannot claim it (terminal, not brief)

Language ownership is cheaper than feature ownership and harder to displace.
