## Sections (scroll — one job each)

### 1. Morning Executive Brief
- **Headline:** What changed overnight against your outcomes  
- **Body:** Three ranked attention items (demand · capacity · capital)  
- **CTA:** Open Priority Decisions  

### 2. Organisation Health
- Enterprise pulse strip (calm)  
- Outcomes on / off trajectory  
- No sparkline carnival  

### 3. Factory Health
| Plant | Load | Frozen horizon | Signal |
|-------|------|----------------|--------|
| Plant 1 | 86% | Stable | Watch |
| Plant 2 | 104% | At risk | Attention |
| Plant 3 | 72% | Stable | Clear |

### 4. Dealer Demand
- Heat cues by region / branch (not a full GIS product)  
- “West branches: Model H pull vs fill”  
- Link → Demand Planning Workspace  

### 5. Production Capacity
- Order bank vs build slots  
- Binding constraint labelled (Plant 2)  
- Link → Factory Ordering Workspace  

### 6. Inventory
- FG / WIP / parts posture  
- Ageing callout (variant)  
- Service vs capital one-liner  

### 7. Working Capital
- Days inventory · cash at risk in ageing  
- CFO altitude — not GL detail  

### 8. Executive Value
- Why this morning’s clarity matters (time · decision · shared picture)  
- No fake ROI guarantees  

### 9. Priority Decisions
1. Re-sequence Plant 2 for West Model H (Council-ready)  
2. Inventory reposition vs H-XL liquidation  
3. Publish dealer allocation rationale
