# Part 2 — Logo Exploration

*30+ concepts across 10 families. The Design Bible recommended "The Meridian." The studio challenges that recommendation.*

---

## Evaluation Framework

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Timelessness | 25% | Will this feel dated in 2036? |
| Enterprise appeal | 20% | Appropriate in a board deck beside McKinsey? |
| Memorability | 15% | Can you draw it from memory after one viewing? |
| App icon | 15% | Legible at 16px? Distinct on home screen? |
| Global scalability | 10% | Works in Tokyo, Dubai, São Paulo without cultural collision? |
| Differentiation | 15% | Distinct from Linear, Notion, Copilot, Bloomberg? |

## Top Five Recommendations

| Rank | Concept | Mark | Why |
|------|---------|------|-----|
| **1** | **23 — The Ledger Line** | ═ parallel rules | Institutional trust, audit narrative, timeless, unique in SaaS, connects to Decision Register |
| **2** | **27 — The Modular E** | Three-rectangle E | Systematic, Swiss precision, app-icon strong, Operating System alignment |
| **3** | **12 — The Seal Ring** | Circle + horizontal | Premium, global, signet authority without tech aesthetic |
| **4** | **07 — The Ligature EO** | E/O connected | Memorable at scale, luxury craft signal, strong monogram equity |
| **5** | **02 — The Period** | Single dot | Radical restraint; definitive; unforgettable if committed to fully |

**Meridian (Concept 01) ranks #8 overall** — correct but not iconic. Safe choices don't build $10B brands.

### Studio Recommendation

**Primary mark:** Ledger Line (23)  
**Wordmark:** ExecutiveOS in custom institutional sans (see Typography exploration)  
**App icon:** Ledger Line on warm institutional background (not pure black — see Colour exploration)  
**Merchandise:** Seal Ring (12) as emboss stamp on physical goods  
**Marketing hero:** Drop Cap E (29) for editorial campaigns only  

**Dual mark system:** Ledger Line (product) + Seal Ring (formal/board exports) — analogous to Apple's apple vs. Apple wordmark for different contexts.
