## Organisational learning

**Purpose:** Improve future judgement from past decisions.

**Loops (Argyris):**

- **Single-loop:** correct errors within existing norms  
- **Double-loop:** question governing variables / strategy  

**Practices:** decision journals; after-action reviews; blameless post-incident reviews with system fixes; kill criteria reviews.

---

## Adaptive leadership (Heifetz)

Distinguish **technical problems** (known solutions) from **adaptive challenges** (require learning and value trade-offs).

Executives fail when they treat adaptive challenges as technical (buy a tool, hire a consultant, declare victory).

---

## Critical thinking in groups

- Protect dissent  
- Separate advocacy from inquiry (Senge)  
- Use “I need your help to find what’s wrong with this”)  
- Record minority opinions  

---

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

Learning rules in EIPF and Council learning panels implement double-loop learning. Reality Lab failure conditions should feed pack refinement — not vanity pass rates.
