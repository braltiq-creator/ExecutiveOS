**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## Industry structure

Extraction of minerals/metals/energy commodities via open pit, underground, or hybrid operations. Often multi-site, capital-intensive, long asset lives. Stakeholders include governments, communities, offtakers, and joint-venture partners.

---

## Value chain

```
Exploration → Development → Extraction → Processing → Logistics → Offtake / Market → Closure & rehab
```

---

## Economic drivers

- Commodity price and FX  
- Grade, recovery, and strip ratios  
- Capex cycle timing  
- Unit cash cost (AISC / C1 as applicable)  
- Licence to operate (ESG, community, safety)  
- Volume vs margin under price swings
