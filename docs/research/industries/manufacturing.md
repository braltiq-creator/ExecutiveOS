## Industry structure

Discrete and process manufacturers spanning OEM, contract manufacturing, and hybrid models. Multi-plant networks common. Channels include direct, dealer/distributor, and OEM supply. Capital intensity and working-capital intensity both high.

---

## Value chain

```
Suppliers → Parts/Commodities → Factories/Lines → FG Inventory → Dealers/Customers → Service/Aftermarket
                ↑________________ Demand signals / Order bank / Allocation ________________↑
```
