# 09 — Manufacturing Benchmarks

**EIPF:** `benchmarks()`  
**Nature:** Reference frames for executive judgement — not league-table vanity.

---

## Benchmark principles

1. Compare like with like (discrete OEM / multi-plant / dealer channel).  
2. Prefer **bands** over single-point targets for utilisation and dealer DIO.  
3. Internal trend often beats external peer when strategy mix differs.  
4. Benchmarks inform Council challenge; they do not auto-decide.

## Internal benchmark types

| Type | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Plant-vs-plant** | Utilisation, OEE, overtime, quality |
| **Region-vs-region** | Forecast bias, dealer fill, lost sales |
| **Model-vs-model** | Margin, turn, strategy mix contribution |
| **Period-vs-plan** | Board and ELT accountability |

---

## How Council uses benchmarks

| Role | Challenge pattern |
|------|-------------------|
| CFO | “Why is DIO 20 days above peer median while fill rate is not?” |
| COO | “Utilisation is top quartile only via overtime — that is not excellence.” |
| CRO | “Dealer DIO is high while fill is low — allocation is broken.” |
| CSO | “Share gains that destroy contribution vs peers are not strategy.” |
| CEO | “Which benchmark gap is Organisation Health, not local KPI theatre?” |

## Reality Lab scoring hooks

Scenarios may score:

- Distance from peer median after recommended action (directional)  
- Whether recommendation cites benchmark context  
- Whether band KPIs are treated as bands (not maximised blindly)
