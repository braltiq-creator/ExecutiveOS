## Studio Hybrid System (Recommended)

Combine #07 + #04 + #09 into a **tri-mode system:**

| Mode | Background | Primary | Use |
|------|------------|---------|-----|
| **Institutional** (default) | `#F7F8FA` | `#1A1F2E` | Daily product |
| **Briefing** (morning) | `#FFF1E5` | `#231F20` | Intelligence Center hero, 6–10am auto |
| **Study** (dark) | `#1E1C1A` | `#E8E6E3` | Evening, travel, user preference |

**Challenge to Design Bible:** Single light-mode Ink 900 system is insufficient. The morning brief deserves the warmth of print journalism. The product deserves institutional navy, not developer black.
