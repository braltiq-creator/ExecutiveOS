# CS-01 — Inventory Denial

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Context

Manufacturing and retail organisations facing softening demand often continue building or buying to “make the plan,” converting forecast error into inventory and later markdowns or write-offs.

---

## What happened *(pattern)*

- Forecast bias stays optimistic  
- Factories/purchasing keep rate  
- Channel or DC inventory swells  
- Clearance/write-offs arrive as “surprise”  
- Board trust erodes  

Public analogues appear repeatedly across cyclical manufacturers and retailers (specific firm names omitted; pattern is the research object).

---

## Executive failure mode

- Treating sell-in as demand  
- Local utilisation goals over cash  
- Avoiding mid-cycle forecast reset  
- Clearance without margin floor  

---

## What exceptional leaders do differently

- Reset demand with ranges early  
- Cost destock options against service floor  
- Make CCC a CEO conversation  
- Pre-commit markdown/write-off language  

---

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

Pack rules: no build on bad forecast; service floor on destock; board honesty on inventory. Reality Lab scenarios: inventory bloat + board challenge.
