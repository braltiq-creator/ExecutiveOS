# Executive Health Model — Field Services

Business Pulse evaluates seven health dimensions:

1. Revenue Health
2. Operational Health
3. People Health
4. Customer Health
5. Cash Flow Health
6. Execution Health
7. Growth Health

Every dimension includes:

- Reasoning
- Evidence
- Confidence
- Trend
- Predicted Direction

## Executive language examples

- Technician utilisation is constraining revenue growth.
- SLA compliance has improved despite increased workload.
- Cash collection has weakened after project completion.
- Gross margin erosion is now the primary strategic risk.

Operational jargon is avoided. Communication stays at executive level.
