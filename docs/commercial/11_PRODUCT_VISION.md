## Five-year vision

**ExecutiveOS becomes the default Executive Intelligence Platform for leadership teams who run serious organisations.**

In five years:

- Executives start the day in ExecutiveOS the way they once started in email — because it is where **attention is ranked** and **judgement is prepared**  
- Outcomes, decisions, and organisational memory compound into a system of record for **executive judgement**  
- Industry context arrives through **Intelligence Packs**, not bespoke platform forks  
- Braltiq is recognised — calmly — as the company that defined the category  

Investor analogy (rare use): Salesforce for revenue systems of record · ExecutiveOS for executive judgement continuity.

## Future Executive Intelligence Packs

Manufacturing is the first production industry pack. The long-term model is a **library of packs** — each extending industry vocabulary, outcomes, events, and Council knowledge **without customising Core**.

Future pack domains (illustrative direction — not a ship commitment):

- Adjacent industrial and heavy-asset verticals  
- Regulated professional services  
- Healthcare operations leadership  
- Financial services division leadership  

**Commercial expression:** Additional Profiles / packs as expansion SKUs when product and packaging are ready ([`02_PRICING_AND_PACKAGING.md`](./02_PRICING_AND_PACKAGING.md)).

## Autonomous Executive Intelligence

**Definition we will use:**

> Autonomous Executive Intelligence means the platform can **continuously observe, rank, and prepare** judgement — including background scans and delegated follow-ups — while **authority and accountability remain with the executive**.

**What it is not:**

- Replacement of the CEO or ELT  
- Unsupervised action on company systems without governance  
- “AI runs the company” marketing  

Release 2.0+ may deepen background and delegated workflows ([`10_PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md`](./10_PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md); [`../business/ROADMAP.md`](../business/ROADMAP.md)). Messaging must stay precise: **autonomy of preparation**, not abdication of leadership.
