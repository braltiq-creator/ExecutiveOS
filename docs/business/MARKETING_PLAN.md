# ExecutiveOS Marketing Plan

## Marketing Objective (Beta → GA)

**Generate 30 qualified beta conversations → 10 beta orgs → 3 paying customers at GA → pipeline for 20 GA prospects.**

Marketing supports founder-led sales; no paid acquisition until $500K ARR.

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1 — Stealth Beta (Weeks 1–4)

- 5 warm-intro design partners onboarded
- No public launch
- Founder LinkedIn posts (2×/week) — executive operating rhythm thought leadership
- Collect usage data + first testimonials

### Phase 2 — Controlled Beta Launch (Weeks 5–8)

- **Announcement:** "ExecutiveOS beta — Executive Intelligence Platform"
- Channels: LinkedIn, email to waitlist, PE/VC ops intros
- **PR:** Not primary; one exclusive if TechCrunch-tier inbound
- **Webinar:** "The End of the Manual Executive Brief" — live Intelligence Center demo

### Phase 3 — GA Launch (Week 12–16 post-beta start)

- Case studies published (2 minimum)
- Pricing page live
- Product Hunt (secondary — executive audience weak; do for SEO backlinks only)
- Paid LinkedIn retargeting ($2K/month test)

## LinkedIn Strategy

**Founder profile is the primary channel.** Company page secondary.

### Content Pillars (2 posts/week)

| Pillar | Example Topics |
|--------|----------------|
| **Executive operating rhythm** | Why morning briefs fail; decision registers |
| **AI for leadership (not workers)** | Why Copilot isn't enough for CEOs |
| **Build in public (beta)** | Graph visualization, advisor orchestration |
| **Customer stories** | CoS time saved (anonymized until GA) |

### Format Mix

- 40% text posts (contrarian takes)
- 30% carousel (Intelligence Center screenshots)
- 20% short video (60-sec demo clips)
- 10% poll (executive habits)

### Engagement Protocol

- Comment on CoS, CEO, PE operating partner posts daily (15 min)
- Connect with Chiefs of Staff at 200–2,000 employee companies
- No generic "excited to announce" — always teach something
