## Industry structure

Public sector agencies and departments delivering policy, regulation, and services under political mandate. Success measured by outcomes for citizens, stewardship of public funds, and trust — not shareholder returns.

---

## Value chain

```
Mandate/Policy → Programme design → Funding → Delivery (self/partners) → Citizen experience → Assurance & accountability
```
