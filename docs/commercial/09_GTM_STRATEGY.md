## Go-to-market strategy (summary)

Land with **founder-led, evidence-based sales** into executive offices that already feel judgement fog. Package **Executive Intelligence Profiles**, not integrations. Prove habit with Design Partners and trials. Expand to teams and Enterprise on clarity earned. Market as a premium category — calm, exact, never loud.

```
Warm network → Discovery → Profile demo → Trial / Design Partner
        → Habit proof → Individual or Team
        → Expansion (profiles · seats · Enterprise)
```

## Product-led growth

| PLG surface | Behaviour |
|-------------|-----------|
| **30-day free trial** | Self-serve provisioning + Discovery |
| **One Profile included** | Clear choice; fast time-to-value |
| **No setup / implementation fee** | Remove procurement theatre |
| **Organisation Portal** | Customer controls subscription and systems |
| **In-product upgrade** | Trial → paid; Individual → Team |

PLG does **not** mean viral freemium for the whole company. It means an executive can start alone and expand the leadership circle with evidence.

## Expansion strategy

1. **Land** — Individual or Team on one Profile  
2. **Habit** — WAEx and morning ritual  
3. **Widen** — More executives on Team  
4. **Deepen** — Additional Profiles / packs when SKUs exist  
5. **Elevate** — Enterprise for multi-entity and security
