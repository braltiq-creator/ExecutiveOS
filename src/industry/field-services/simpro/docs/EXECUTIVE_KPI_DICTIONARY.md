# Executive KPI Dictionary — Field Services

| KPI | Unit | Polarity | Executive meaning |
|-----|------|----------|-------------------|
| Technician Utilisation | % | higher* | Labour deployment vs capacity (*too high = strain) |
| First-Time Fix Rate | % | higher | Quality of first-visit resolution |
| Revenue per Technician | $ | higher | Workforce commercial productivity |
| Gross Margin | % | higher | Whether growth converts to value |
| Quote Conversion | % | higher | Quote → accepted work effectiveness |
| Average Response Time | hours | lower | Speed to customer demand |
| SLA Compliance | % | higher | Reliability vs contracted commitments |
| Preventive vs Reactive | ratio | higher | Mix quality / margin stability |
| Job Backlog | count | lower | Unfinished demand in the system |
| Work In Progress | $ | lower | Capital locked in unfinished work |
| Cash Collection | % | higher | Invoice → cash conversion |
| Recurring Revenue | $ | higher | Contracted service base |
| Customer Concentration | % | lower | Dependency on few logos |
| Schedule Efficiency | % | higher | Roster → productive field time |
| Travel Time | minutes | lower | Non-productive movement |
| Labour Recovery | % | higher | Labour cost recovered billably |
| Variation Recovery | % | higher | Scope change → recognised value |
| Project Profitability | % | higher | Major projects create/destroy margin |

\* Utilisation above ~95% is treated as a people/growth risk, not a win.
