## What the customer is buying

### Executive Intelligence Profile (included — choose one)

| Profile | Intent |
|---------|--------|
| **Operations Executive** | Delivery, capacity, operational resilience |
| **Commercial Executive** | Pipeline, revenue system, commercial confidence |
| **Manufacturing Executive** | Demand, factory, inventory, dealers, working capital — via Manufacturing Intelligence Pack packaging |

Profiles package how the platform thinks with the executive. Connectors (Microsoft 365, Simpro, Salesforce, Dynamics) are **implementation details** that make Discovery and context richer — they are not the SKU.

### What every paid package includes (capability framing)

Speak in outcomes, not feature dumps:

- Command Centre / Executive Brief  
- Strategy · Decision · Knowledge · Operating Loop  
- Executive Council (role-consistent judgement)  
- Organisation Portal (subscription, security, systems, value)  
- Self-service provisioning and onboarding (Discovery)  
- Industry context when the selected profile requires a pack (e.g. Manufacturing)  

Enterprise may add contractual items (residency, SSO path, support SLAs, multi-pack / multi-profile) — never invent features not on the roadmap.

## Why this pricing exists

1. **Altitude pricing.** We sell to the people who run the company. Value is measured in clearer judgement and reclaimed executive time — not seats for the whole workforce.  
2. **Premium category.** Executive Intelligence is closer to vertical decision infrastructure than horizontal AI add-ons ($30–$60/user). List price reflects that altitude.  
3. **Simple packaging.** Three packages. One profile included. No implementation tax. Friction is cognitive, not procurement theatre.  
4. **Land with honesty.** Individual Executive lets a single leader prove the rhythm. Executive Team is the natural home when Council and shared outcomes matter.  
5. **Expand on intelligence.** Expansion is more profiles, more packs, more executives — not forced connector upsells.  

**Value anchors (use in conversation, not as guarantees):**

- Hours reclaimed from brief assembly and context switching  
- Faster resolution of pending decisions  
- Shared picture across the leadership team  
- Industry-aware Council without a custom build

## Expansion strategy

| Motion | Trigger | Next step |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Individual → Team** | Second executive needs the same picture | Upgrade to Executive Team |
| **Profile expansion** | New domain of judgement (e.g. Commercial after Operations) | Add profile / pack (future SKU) |
| **Team → Enterprise** | Multi-entity, residency, security review, volume | Custom Enterprise |
| **Industry pack** | Manufacturing / future packs | Profile or pack add-on (see roadmap) |
| **Seat / executive growth** | More leaders active in Portal | Team or Enterprise true-up |

**Rule:** Never expand by frightening the customer with connector line items. Expand when judgement value is proven.

## Competitive price frame (honest)

| Alternative | Approx. cost | Why ExecutiveOS still wins the conversation |
|-------------|--------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Copilot add-on | ~USD $30/user | Different job — task assistance vs judgement infrastructure |
| ChatGPT Enterprise | ~USD $60/user | Answers ≠ executive operating rhythm |
| Human CoS time | Material salary + overhead | We amplify humans; we don’t claim to replace them |
| Consulting sprint | Project fees | Episodic vs continuous |

We do **not** win by being cheaper than Copilot per seat. We win by owning a different job-to-be-done.

## Trial and conversion

1. Start Free Trial (self-serve provisioning)  
2. Choose Executive Profile  
3. Complete Discovery (role, three outcomes, briefing time)  
4. Connect systems when ready (optional for first value)  
5. Day 25–30: upgrade prompt from Organisation Portal / trial management  
6. Credit card → Individual or Team; Enterprise → sales-assisted  

No setup fee. No implementation fee. White-glove remains a Design Partner privilege, not a tax on every customer.
