# CS-05 — Growth at All Costs

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Context

Firms chase revenue, users, or share while unit economics, conduct, reliability, or culture deteriorate. Corrections arrive via capital markets, regulators, or customer revolt.

---

## What happened *(pattern)*

- Vanity growth metrics dominate  
- Discounting or subsidies hide weak demand  
- Controls and culture lag scale  
- Trust or capital access breaks  
- Forced restructuring  

Seen across tech hypergrowth, financial services misconduct cycles, and channel-stuffed industrials.

---

## Executive failure mode

- Celebrating bookings over quality of revenue  
- Ignoring CAC/retention/contribution  
- Silencing risk and finance  
- Confusing narrative with economics  

---

## What exceptional leaders do differently

- Define quality-of-growth metrics  
- Time-box subsidies  
- Pair CRO incentives with retention/margin  
- Keep CFO/Risk dissent loud  

---

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

Business value and trust dimensions in evaluation; CRO commitment quality; Rule-of-40 style metrics used critically in technology research.
