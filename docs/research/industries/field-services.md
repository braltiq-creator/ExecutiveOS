## Industry structure

Organisations delivering installed-base service, maintenance, projects, and trades work at customer sites. Includes HVAC, electrical, facilities, industrial maintenance, and utilities field forces. Systems of record often include FSM platforms (e.g. Simpro and peers).

---

## Value chain

```
Lead/Contract → Quote → Schedule → Dispatch → Execute job/project → Invoice → Collect → Renew/Upsell
```
