# ExecutiveOS Customer Onboarding Playbook

## Onboarding Philosophy

ExecutiveOS value compounds with **executive artifacts** — objectives, decisions, initiatives, memory. Onboarding is not "connect and forget"; it is a structured **8-week activation** to populate the executive graph and establish daily Intelligence Center habit.

**Owner:** CEO/founder for beta; Customer Success hire at v1.0  
**Success definition:** Executive opens Intelligence Center ≥4 days/week and logs ≥1 decision by Week 2.

---

## Day 0 — Pre-Launch (Before First Login)

| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 0.1 | Send welcome email with beta expectations, calendar link for kickoff | CEO |
| 0.2 | Confirm M365 admin consent path for calendar integration | CoS + IT |
| 0.3 | Identify executive sponsor (CEO) and day-to-day champion (CoS) | CEO |
| 0.4 | Collect: company name, industry, top 3 strategic objectives (pre-fill) | CoS |
| 0.5 | Create organization + invite seats (CEO, CoS, +2 execs minimum) | ExecutiveOS |
| 0.6 | Share 2-min loom: "Your first 10 minutes in ExecutiveOS" | ExecutiveOS |

**Deliverable:** Kickoff call scheduled within 48 hours of account creation.

---

## Day 1 — Executive Activation

**Kickoff call (45 min)**

| Time | Agenda |
|------|--------|
| 0–5 | Vision: proactive intelligence, not another chatbot |
| 5–15 | Live tour: Intelligence Center → Graph → Advisors |
| 15–25 | Guided onboarding wizard (profile, objectives, challenges) |
| 25–35 | Connect Microsoft 365 calendar |
| 35–40 | Log first decision together |
| 40–45 | Set Week 1 homework (see below) |

**Day 1 homework (CoS champion):**
- [ ] Complete executive profile + 3 objectives
- [ ] Log 2 decisions from last quarter
- [ ] Create 2 strategic initiatives with target dates
- [ ] Capture 3 executive memory entries (1 risk, 1 opportunity, 1 insight)
- [ ] CEO opens Intelligence Center tomorrow morning before email

---

## Week 1 — Foundation (Graph Population)

**Goal:** Knowledge graph ≥20 nodes; Intelligence Center shows ≥3 prioritized cards.

| Day | Focus | Actions |
|-----|-------|---------|
| Mon | Objectives + initiatives | Align initiatives to objectives |
| Tue | Decisions | Log 3 pending decisions with review dates |
| Wed | Memory | Capture leadership meeting notes as memory |
| Thu | Calendar | Verify today's agenda + meeting prep appears |
| Fri | Check-in (15 min async) | Pulse survey + blockers |

**Week 1 success milestones:**
- [ ] All invited execs signed in
- [ ] M365 calendar synced
- [ ] ≥3 initiatives with health visible
- [ ] Intelligence Center shows critical attention OR risks card populated
- [ ] 1 advisor consultation completed

---

## Week 2 — Operating Rhythm

**Goal:** Establish daily Intelligence Center habit for CEO.

| Ritual | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Morning brief** | CEO opens `/dashboard` before first meeting (digest auto-selected) |
| **Decision Friday** | Log decisions made this week every Friday |
| **CoS graph review** | CoS expands graph nodes for top initiative |

**Enable meeting intelligence (Professional+):**
- Walk through `/calendar` meeting prep
- Validate prep links to initiatives/decisions

**Week 2 success milestones:**
- [ ] CEO opened Intelligence Center ≥4 days
- [ ] ≥5 decisions in register
- [ ] CoS reports ≥1 hr saved on brief assembly
- [ ] Week 2 activation interview completed

---

## Week 4 — Depth + Health Analytics

**Goal:** Multi-advisor usage; Executive plan features validated (Executive slots).

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| Advisors | Run one collaborative query (strategy + risk) before board/exec session |
| Health | Review portfolio health score with CEO; discuss declining initiatives |
| Graph | Execute quick query: "risks blocking [objective]" |
| Team | Invite CFO or CRO; domain advisor demo (Financial or Sales) |

**Week 4 success milestones:**
- [ ] ≥2 execs using advisors weekly
- [ ] Health analytics reviewed in exec session (Executive plan)
- [ ] NPS pulse ≥30
- [ ] Week 4 checkpoint met (`BETA_PROGRAM.md` criteria)

---

## Week 8 — Value Proof + Conversion

**Goal:** Document ROI; secure GA conversion intent.

| Activity | Output |
|----------|--------|
| ROI worksheet | Hours saved × loaded rate |
| Exit interview | NPS + conversion likelihood |
| Case study draft | Problem → usage → outcome |
| Conversion offer | 50% Year 1 beta pricing |

**Week 8 success milestones:**
- [ ] Documented ROI ≥3:1 vs. plan price
- [ ] NPS ≥40
- [ ] Conversion intent: Likely or Very Likely
- [ ] Reference agreement signed (if NPS ≥9)

---

## Executive Activation Checklist

### CEO (Economic Buyer)

- [ ] Completed onboarding (profile, objectives)
- [ ] Opens Intelligence Center ≥4 days/week
- [ ] Reviews top 3 priority cards weekly
- [ ] Consulted Chief of Staff advisor ≥1×
- [ ] Attended Week 4 health review

### Chief of Staff (Champion)

- [ ] M365 connected + calendar verified
- [ ] ≥10 decisions logged
- [ ] ≥5 initiatives tracked with owners
- [ ] ≥10 memory entries captured
- [ ] Graph rebuilt after major data adds
- [ ] Meeting prep used before ≥2 exec meetings
- [ ] Weekly pulse survey completed

### CFO / CRO (Secondary Exec)

- [ ] Signed in + profile complete
- [ ] Used domain advisor (Financial or Sales) ≥2×
- [ ] Initiative linked to their function

## Success Milestones Summary

| Milestone | When | Signal |
|-----------|------|--------|
| **Activated** | Day 3 | Onboarding complete + calendar connected |
| **Engaged** | Week 2 | ≥4 Intelligence Center days/week |
| **Embedded** | Week 4 | Advisors + graph in workflow |
| **Proven** | Week 8 | ROI documented + NPS ≥40 |
| **Converted** | Week 8–10 | Paid contract signed |

---

## Common Failure Modes + Interventions

| Failure | Signal | Intervention |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Empty dashboard** | No objectives/initiatives | 30-min data population session |
| **CEO non-adoption** | CoS only user | CEO-specific 15-min daily ritual call |
| **Calendar not connected** | No meeting prep | IT admin call; send consent guide |
| **AI distrust** | Low advisor usage | Show graph-backed citations in responses |
| **Tool fatigue** | Low WAEx Week 3 | Narrow to Intelligence Center only for 1 week |
