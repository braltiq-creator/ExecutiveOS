# Business Event Catalogue — Simpro → ExecutiveOS

| Simpro event | Executive meaning | Health impacts |
|--------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Quote Accepted | Revenue Opportunity Increased | revenue, growth |
| Quote Lost | Revenue Opportunity Decreased | revenue, growth |
| Job Delayed | Delivery Risk Increased | operational, execution, customer |
| Job Completed | Execution Progress Increased | execution, operational |
| Technician Unavailable | Capacity Reduced | people, operational, growth |
| Purchase Order Delayed | Supply Chain Risk Increased | operational, execution |
| Invoice Overdue | Cash Flow Risk Increased | cash, revenue |
| Major Customer Complaint | Customer Health Reduced | customer, growth |
| Contract Renewed | Recurring Revenue Increased | revenue, growth, customer |
| Contract Cancelled | Recurring Revenue Decreased | revenue, customer, growth |
| Variation Approved | Variation Recovery Increased | revenue, execution |
| Defect Raised | Quality Risk Increased | customer, operational, execution |
| SLA Breached | Customer Commitment Risk Increased | customer, operational |
| Timesheet Overtime | People Strain Increased | people, operational |
| Stock Shortage | Supply Constraint Increased | operational, execution |
| Project Margin Eroded | Gross Margin Risk Increased | revenue, execution, growth |

All mappings emit canonical `BusinessEvent` records. Intelligence never receives Simpro payloads.
