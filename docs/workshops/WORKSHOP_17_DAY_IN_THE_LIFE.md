# Workshop 17 — A Day in the Life of an Executive

**Type:** Facilitated design workshop (founders)  
**Duration:** Suggested 90–120 minutes  
**Primary lens:** Growing Business Leader (10–100 employees)  
**Output:** Completed decisions in §8 (Founder Decisions)  
**Not in scope:** Designing the experience · answering questions in advance · UX · wireframes · mock-ups · implementation  

**Context (read beforehand; do not re-litigate as the workshop):**  
Constitution · Core Executive Loop · Executive Experience Blueprint · Product Strategy v1 · Workshop 16 outcomes (if ratified)  

---

## How to run this workshop

1. Assign a **facilitator** and a **scribe**.  
2. Complete §1 together before touching the timeline.  
3. Walk the day **in order** (§2). Park interruptions/notifications until §3–4 unless a time block forces the issue.  
4. Capture decision moments in §5 as they emerge; do not invent a product roadmap.  
5. End only when §8 has agreed decisions — or parked items with owners.  
6. Prefer verbs and situations over feature names.
