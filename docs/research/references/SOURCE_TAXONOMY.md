## Categories

| Category | Description | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **A — Canonical books** | Widely taught management / decision works | Drucker, Rumelt, Kahneman, Goldratt |
| **B — Academic research** | Peer-reviewed or equivalent rigor | Edmondson psychological safety; forecasting research |
| **C — Professional bodies / standards** | Formal standards and bodies of knowledge | ISO 31000, COSO, ASCM, NIST CSF, ICMM |
| **D — Industry practice** | Practitioner frameworks with field validation | S&OP/IBP, MEDDICC, SRE, Stage-Gate |
| **E — Regulatory / governance** | Codes and prudential regimes | Basel, local conduct regulators, Green Book |
| **F — Case patterns** | Recurring public historical patterns (anonymised in EIRL) | Inventory denial; risk silence |
| **G — ExecutiveOS synthesis** | Original Braltiq integration — **not** established theory | Council lenses; Focus Outcomes; operating loop; EIPF mapping |

---

## Citation rules

1. Prefer primary or widely recognised secondary sources.  
2. Label commercial data vendors as commercial — do not present as academic.  
3. Never present **G — synthesis** as if it were established science.  
4. When industry stats are illustrative design anchors, say so (see manufacturing benchmarks blueprint).  

---

## Quality bar for adding sources

- Relevant to executive judgement or industry structure  
- Traceable  
- Not solely a vendor brochure  
- Updates document version when added materially
