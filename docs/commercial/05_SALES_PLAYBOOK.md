## Ideal customer

**Primary:** Executive office of a mid-market company (≈200–2,000 employees, ≈AUD $50M–$500M revenue) with a CEO/COO and a Chief of Staff or senior EA who still assembles leadership intelligence by hand.

**Secondary:** Division leadership in larger enterprises evaluating an Executive Intelligence layer above M365 / CRM / ERP — especially Operations, Commercial, or Manufacturing profiles.

Detail: [`../business/IDEAL_CUSTOMER_PROFILE.md`](../business/IDEAL_CUSTOMER_PROFILE.md). Geography first: Australia, UK, US (English-first).

## Qualification

### Must have

- Executive sponsor (CEO/COO or equivalent)  
- Someone who owns the operating rhythm (CoS, EA, or the executive themselves)  
- Willingness to define **three outcomes** in Discovery  
- English-speaking; timezone overlap for white-glove where needed  

### Strong signals

- Manual morning briefs / board pack assembly  
- Failed or shallow Copilot / ChatGPT executive adoption  
- New CEO in first 90 days  
- Board pressure on execution visibility  
- Manufacturing / industrial OEM with dealer + factory tension (Manufacturing Profile)  

### Disqualify

- No executive sponsor  
- Expectation of on-premise deployment  
- Buying “integrations as the product” with no appetite for judgement workflow  
- Pure individual productivity tool seeker with no organisational outcomes

## Discovery (Call 1 — 30 minutes)

**Goals:** Qualify · name champion and buyer · quantify pain · choose likely Profile.

### Questions (use sparingly — listen more)

**Situation**  
1. How does the CEO (or accountable executive) start the day today?  
2. Who assembles leadership context — CoS, EA, strategy?  
3. Which three outcomes would you defend to the board this quarter?  
4. What is the executive stack — M365, Salesforce, Dynamics, Simpro, other?

**Pain**  
5. How long does weekly exec brief or board prep take?  
6. When did a decision get lost or re-debated because context was missing?  
7. What happened when Copilot or ChatGPT was rolled out to leadership?

**Impact**  
8. If the executive reclaimed two hours a week, where would that time go?  
9. What is the cost of learning initiative health only at quarterly review?

**Decision**  
10. Who signs leadership tooling?  
11. Timeline for improving operating rhythm?  
12. Preference: Individual Executive, Executive Team, or Enterprise conversation?

**Exit of call:** Profile hypothesis (Operations / Commercial / Manufacturing) + demo booked with the economic buyer or champion + sponsor.

## Commercial conversations

### Frame the purchase

> You are buying **Executive Intelligence** — a Profile that shapes how the platform thinks with you. Systems you connect make Discovery richer. They are not the SKU.

### Value anchors (not guarantees)

- Hours reclaimed from assembly work  
- Faster resolution of pending decisions  
- Shared picture across the leadership team  
- Industry-aware Council without a custom platform  

### ROI language (update numbers to AUD packaging)

Compare to **Chief of Staff time** and **decision latency**, not Copilot seat price. Example framing:

> Executive Team is AUD $1,995/month. If your CoS reclaims five hours a week at a conservative loaded rate, the platform pays for itself before counting clearer judgement.

Individual Executive is the land motion for a single accountable leader proving the rhythm.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response direction |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **“We have Copilot.”** | Copilot assists inside M365. ExecutiveOS runs the executive operating rhythm above systems of record. They can coexist. |
| **“We use ChatGPT.”** | Chat answers. ExecutiveOS maintains outcomes, decisions, overnight change, and Council judgement. |
| **“Another dashboard.”** | Dashboards wait. We rank what requires judgement against outcomes — then prepare the decision. |
| **“Execs won’t adopt.”** | Value is proactive — Command Centre before email. Habit is the product metric. |
| **“Security / AI data.”** | Org-scoped isolation, contractual no-training posture, security page walkthrough for CISO. |
| **“Too expensive vs Copilot.”** | Different job. Compare to CoS time and decision cost, not per-seat AI add-ons. |
| **“Build internally.”** | Graph, Council, packs, provisioning, Portal — years of productisation. Time-to-value is days via trial. |
| **“We need consultants.”** | Consultants are episodic. ExecutiveOS is continuous judgement infrastructure. Partner, don’t replace, when needed. |
| **“Integrations first.”** | Connect when ready. First value is outcomes + Profile + Command Centre — not a connector project. |
