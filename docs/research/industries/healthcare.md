# Healthcare — Industry Intelligence Research

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Industry code:** `healthcare`

---

## Industry structure

Providers (hospitals, clinics, aged care), payers, and integrated systems. Clinical risk and capacity constraints dominate. Funding models vary: public, private insurance, hybrid.

---

## Value chain

```
Access/Referral → Triage → Care delivery → Diagnostics → Treatment → Discharge/Follow-up → Outcomes & payment
```

---

## Economic drivers

- Capacity (beds, theatres, clinicians)  
- Case mix and reimbursement  
- Length of stay  
- Labour (clinical workforce)  
- Quality/safety events cost  
- Throughput vs acuity
