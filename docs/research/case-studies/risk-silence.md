# CS-04 — Risk Silence

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Context

Organisations punish or socially sanction bad news. Risks go underground until they become board-level crises (safety, conduct, cyber, inventory, credit).

---

## What happened *(pattern)*

- Green dashboards  
- Informal waivers permanent  
- Near-misses unreported  
- External shock or whistleblower surfaces truth  
- Trust collapse with board/regulators/public  

---

## Executive failure mode

- Shoot-the-messenger culture  
- Risk register theatre  
- Optimism in disclosure language  
- No residual risk owners  

---

## What exceptional leaders do differently

- Reward early escalation  
- Require ranged narratives  
- Expire exceptions  
- Protect speak-up channels  

---

## Implications for ExecutiveOS *(synthesis)*

Board surprise as Reality Lab failure; psychological safety research; Risk officer overlays; honest pulse language.
