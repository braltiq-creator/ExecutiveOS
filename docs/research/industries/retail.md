## Industry structure

Merchandise and retail services across physical, digital, and omnichannel. Economics driven by traffic, conversion, basket, margin, and inventory turn. Promo and markdown discipline often decide profit.

---

## Value chain

```
Plan → Source → Inbound → Allocate/Replenish → Sell (store/digital) → Fulfil → Return → Learn
```

---

## Economic drivers

- Like-for-like sales
