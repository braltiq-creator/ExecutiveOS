# ExecutiveOS CEO Playbook — 90-Day Beta Launch Operating Plan

## Your Role

You are CEO, product owner, and primary seller. For 90 days, **nothing matters except 10 successful beta orgs, 3 paid conversions, and proof that executives open ExecutiveOS daily.**

Engineering supports; marketing amplifies; customers validate.

---

## 90-Day Objectives

| Day | Milestone |
|-----|-----------|
| **30** | 5 beta orgs activated; WAEx ≥60%; first NPS baseline |
| **60** | 10 beta orgs live; 2 case studies drafted; pipeline of 10 GA prospects |
| **90** | 3 paid conversions; GA date set; v1.0 scope locked; $X pre-orders or LOIs |

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

### Monday — Metrics + Priorities (2 hr)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 8:00 | Review WAEx, activation, advisor usage, P0 bugs (`METRICS.md`) |
| 8:30 | Set 3 company priorities for the week (write them down) |
| 9:00 | Product review: ship list for the week (max 5 items) |
| 9:30 | Sales pipeline review: beta slots filled? follow-ups due? |

**Output:** Weekly priorities posted to team (Slack/email).

### Tuesday — Customer (4 hr)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| AM | 2× beta customer calls (30 min each) — discovery or check-in |
| PM | 1× prospect demo (45 min) OR beta onboarding kickoff |
| PM | Send follow-up emails same day (recap + next steps) |

**Output:** CRM notes (even if spreadsheet): pain, usage, blockers, conversion intent.

### Wednesday — Product + Engineering (3 hr)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| AM | Bug triage with engineering — P0 same-day, P1 this week |
| PM | Review shipped fixes; test Intelligence Center yourself |
| PM | 1× "dogfood" session — use ExecutiveOS as your own CoS |

**Output:** Shipped fixes visible to beta users by Thursday demo.

### Thursday — External (3 hr)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| AM | LinkedIn post (schedule 2/week total) + 15 min engagement |
| AM | Optional: group beta office hours (30 min drop-in) |
| PM | PE/VC ops outreach — 3 emails for beta intros |
| PM | Content: work on case study, blog, or sales asset |

### Friday — Feedback + Retro (2 hr)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| AM | Review weekly pulse surveys — identify #1 friction |
| AM | Send beta users summary: "You said X → we're shipping Y" |
| PM | Internal retro (30 min): what worked, what didn't |
| PM | Plan next week customer calls |

## Decision Framework

When facing any decision, score options:

| Filter | Question |
|--------|----------|
| **Beta impact** | Does this help 10 orgs succeed in 8 weeks? |
| **Revenue path** | Does this unblock conversion or GA? |
| **Focus cost** | Does this distract from customer calls? |
| **Reversible?** | Can we undo in 1 week? |

**Default yes:** Customer-requested fixes, onboarding improvements, security docs  
**Default no:** New modules, new integrations (unless Enterprise beta blocker), rebrand, conference sponsorships

**Escalation:** If 2 beta orgs request same thing → roadmap. If 1 org requests → backlog.

## Risk Register (Review Weekly)

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| CEOs don't adopt (CoS-only usage) | Medium | Critical | CEO ritual in onboarding; exec-specific demo |
| Copilot blocks IT approval | Medium | High | Complement narrative; security one-pager |
| Beta org churns before Week 4 | Medium | High | Week 2 intervention playbook |
| P0 production incident | Low | Critical | `/admin/system` monitoring; on-call founder |
| AI cost overrun | Medium | Medium | Usage limits; monitor `organization_usage` |
| Slow conversion at beta exit | Medium | High | Start ROI docs Week 5; LOI Week 6 |
| Founder burnout | Medium | Critical | Strict weekly rhythm; say no to non-ICP |
| Security review delays Enterprise | High | Medium | Defer Enterprise; focus Primary ICP |
