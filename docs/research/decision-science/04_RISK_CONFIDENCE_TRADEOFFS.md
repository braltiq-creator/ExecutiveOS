## Risk analysis

**Purpose:** Make uncertainty decision-relevant.

**Practices:**

- Separate likelihood, impact, detectability, velocity  
- Stress / reverse stress tests  
- Residual risk with named owners  
- Distinguish aleatory vs epistemic uncertainty  

**Executive trap:** Heat maps without narrative or appetite.

---

## Confidence modelling

Confidence is a **claim about knowledge**, not a synonym for optimism.

| Practice | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Calibration | 80% intervals should contain truth ~80% of the time |
| Ranges | Prefer intervals over points for material forecasts |
| Unknowns list | Explicit missing information |
| Source quality | Freshness, coverage, independence of evidence |

**Superforecasting habits (Tetlock):** update often; use base rates; avoid ideology.

## Trade-off analysis

Every material executive decision allocates pain.

**Pattern:**

1. Name the dimensions (cash, service, share, safety, time, trust)  
2. Score options qualitatively or quantitatively  
3. State who bears the downside  
4. Set review trigger  

**Classic executive trade-offs:** growth vs margin; speed vs certainty; utilisation vs lead time; resilience vs cost; transparency vs panic.
