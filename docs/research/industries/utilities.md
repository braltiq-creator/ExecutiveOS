## Industry structure

Regulated or semi-regulated providers of electricity, gas, water, or multi-utility services. Asset-heavy networks with public service obligations. Revenue often shaped by regulatory determinations.

---

## Value chain

```
Generation/Source → Transmission → Distribution → Retail/Customer → Field ops & restoration
         ↑______________ Asset management & capital programme ______________↑
```
