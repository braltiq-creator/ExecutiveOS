## Voice

ExecutiveOS speaks like the Chief of Staff you trust in the room — not the consultant performing for attention.

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Calm | Loud |
| Confident | Arrogant |
| Intelligent | Clever |
| Premium | Luxurious for its own sake |
| Trustworthy | Salesy |
| Proactive | Alarmist |
| Precise | Robotic |
| Warm (quietly) | Casual |

**Archetype:** Sage + Steward — wisdom without drama; stewardship without bureaucracy.

---

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| **Website hero** | Clear, decisive, short |
| **Product UI** | Sparse, factual, executive-native |
| **Sales** | Curious, respectful, evidence-led |
| **Support** | Helpful, unhurried, precise |
| **Security** | Factual, no fear marketing |
| **Error / failure** | Honest, no blame, next action clear |

---

## Writing principles

1. **Clarity over volume** — One sentence that ranks beats a paragraph that lists.  
2. **Judgement over automation** — Never imply the product decides for the executive.  
3. **Plain language** — Executive-native terms (outcomes, stakes, trade-offs) over jargon.  
4. **Exactness** — Prefer numbers, dates, and named capabilities that exist.  
5. **No hype words** — Avoid revolutionary, magical, game-changing, AI-powered (as decoration).  
6. **Active ownership** — “You decide” remains visible in every major narrative.  
7. **Confidence Through Clarity** — Use as philosophy, not a sticker on every block.

## Typography

Align to the Executive Experience System and design identity docs:

- **Display / brand:** Expressive serif or designated brand display — never Inter/Roboto/Arial as the hero voice  
- **Body:** Readable sans from the design system  
- **Hierarchy:** Few sizes, strong contrast, generous line length limits on marketing  
- **All caps:** Rare; prefer sentence case for executive dignity  

Detail: [`../design/VISUAL_IDENTITY.md`](../design/VISUAL_IDENTITY.md), Experience typography tokens.

---

## Colour philosophy

- Light premium canvas — **not** dark admin UI for customer surfaces  
- Restrained palette; urgency in content, not visual panic  
- Avoid default AI clichés: purple-on-white gradients, glow stacks, neon accents  
- Brand colour used as signal, not wallpaper  
- Product and marketing share EXS / EOS tokens where practical  

---

## Photography

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| Real workplaces, calm leadership environments | Stock “handshake in glass office” clichés |
| Product UI as the visual hero on product pages | Collages of unrelated apps |
| Natural light, restrained grade | Cyberpunk / neon “AI future” |

Photography supports atmosphere; it does not replace the product as proof.

---

## Illustration

- Sparse · geometric · architectural — never cartoon mascots  
- Use to explain Operating Loop or judgement states, not to decorate  
- Prefer product frames when explaining the platform  

---

## Icons

- Minimal line or solid set from Experience System  
- One metaphor per concept  
- Never emoji in executive-facing brand surfaces  

---

## Executive Experience alignment

| Marketing | Product |
|-----------|---------|
| Light premium | Light premium (`AppFrame`, EXS) |
| Calm Command Centre imagery | Actual Command Centre |
| Organisation Portal as customer control | `/organisation` Experience pages |
| No dark “admin console” story | Admin (`/admin`) remains internal |

The website should feel like the same company as the product — Stripe-level continuity, not a separate brand skin.

---

## Brand promise (repeatable)

**You will always know what requires your attention — and why — before your first meeting.**

## Checklist before publishing

- [ ] Would this sentence survive a boardroom?  
- [ ] Does it claim only shipped or clearly roadmap-labelled capability?  
- [ ] Is the CTA calm and clear?  
- [ ] Does removing the logo still feel like ExecutiveOS?
