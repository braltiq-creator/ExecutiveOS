## Workshop B — Logo Blind Test Protocol

1. Display 31 marks at 48px, monochrome, no wordmark, random order
2. Ask: "Which feels most like software a Fortune 500 CEO would trust?"
3. Ask: "Which would you remember tomorrow?"
4. Ask: "Which feels least like AI?"
5. Reveal wordmark pairings for top 5

## Scenario C — The 7am Tuesday

*Full narrative scenario testing Briefing OS hybrid.*

**Executive:** Sarah Chen, CEO, 800-person SaaS company, Series C.  
**Context:** Board meeting today 9am. Initiative Aurora at risk. Two pending decisions. Flight at 4pm.

**6:58am — iPhone, bed:** Opens ExecutiveOS. FT Pink canvas. No cold black. Feels like opening morning paper, not work email.

**6:59am — Lead story:** "Board prep requires review. Risk advisor identified Section 4 exposure on Initiative Aurora timeline. Your 9am with James (GC) may resolve." Sarah thinks: *That's the one thing.*

**7:00am — Also today:** Three single lines. Calendar anchor inline. 30 seconds total.

**7:01am — Close app:** Confidence Exit: "1 decision pending. Initiative Aurora at risk. Board prep at 9." Not alarming — factual.

**8:30am — Desk, MacBook:** Operating mode auto-activated. Panel layout. Priority queue shows Aurora at top. Decision queue shows vendor decision. Calendar panel shows James at 9, board at 11.

**8:45am — Decision:** Vendor decision. Strategy and Risk advisors expanded. Approve Option B. Decision Stamp. 800ms satisfaction.

**9:00am — Meeting with GC:** Opens Board Preview on Initiative Aurora card. AirPlay to room display. ExecutiveOS is presentation tool.

**5:00pm — Airport:** Dark Study mode on iPhone. Quick scan. Nothing new critical.

**5:01pm — Close:** "2 decisions made today. 1 initiative at risk — Aurora. Safe travels."

**Emotional arc:** Prepared → Decisive → In control → Done.

## Scenario D — The Empty State Crisis

*What happens when ExecutiveOS has nothing to say?*

**Danger:** Empty Intelligence Center destroys trust. "This product doesn't know me."

**Studio solution — Empty Brief copy:**

> Your Intelligence Center is quiet today.  
> 4 meetings scheduled. 2 initiatives on track. No pending decisions.  
> This is what calm looks like.

**Never show:** "No insights found." / "Connect more data." / Empty card grid.

**Empty = success.** Train executives that quiet is positive. Confidence Exit on quiet days: "Quiet day. Nothing requires your attention."
