**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## Industry structure

Project-based delivery of buildings and infrastructure by general contractors, specialty trades, and developers. Margin thin; risk concentrated in estimates, claims, and schedule. Labour and subcontracting intensive.

---

## Value chain

```
Develop/Win bid → Design/Precon → Procure → Construct → Commission → Handover → Defects liability
```

---
