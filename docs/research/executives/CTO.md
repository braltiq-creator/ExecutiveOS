**Version:** 1.0.0

**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

---

## Purpose of the role

The CTO owns **technical strategy and product/technology advantage**: architecture choices, engineering effectiveness, and technology bets that create durable differentiation. Exceptional CTOs connect technical decisions to customer and economic outcomes — not fashion.

---

## Primary responsibilities

- Technical vision and architecture strategy
- Engineering organisation effectiveness
- Product-technology roadmap coherence (with CPO/CMO as applicable)
- Build/buy/partner for differentiating capabilities
- Technical risk of product (quality, scale, safety)
- Innovation portfolio with kill criteria

| Model | Use |

## Questions · Signals · Indicators

**Questions:** Does this create advantage or cost? What is the failure mode at 10×? What debt are we consciously accepting? Can we staff this bet?

**Signals:** Deployment frequency, change fail rate, escaped defects, roadmap slip, key-person dependencies  

**Leading:** PR cycle time, review quality, prototype learning velocity  

**Lagging:** NPS of product reliability, incident severity, engineering attrition of top talent, feature ROI  

---

## Rhythms · Success · Warnings

**Rhythms:** Weekly eng health · Monthly architecture · Quarterly tech strategy · Event: sev-1 / security  

**Success:** Differentiating bets ship; commodity bought; reliability improves; talent stays  

**Warnings:** Resume-driven development; endless rewrite; innovation without customers; hero culture  

---

## Disagreements · Behaviours · Board · Style

**Disagreements:** CIO (enterprise systems vs product tech); CRO (feature promises); CFO (cost of platform); CSO (tech as strategy); CEO (timeline ambition)

**Behaviours:** Teaches trade-offs; kills pet projects; elevates reliability; hires for judgement  

**Board:** Technology moat; major programme risk; cyber/product safety  

**Style:** Precise technical translation for executives; diagrams over jargon
