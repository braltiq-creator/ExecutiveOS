# Chief Strategy Officer — Executive Intelligence Research

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Role code:** `cso`

---

## Purpose of the role

The CSO protects **strategic direction and option quality**: where the enterprise will compete, which bets to fund, and whether today’s actions compound or dilute the long game. Exceptional CSOs are not deck factories — they are guardians of choice under uncertainty.

---

## Primary responsibilities

- Strategy formulation and choice architecture
- Portfolio of strategic initiatives / bets
- Competitive and market intelligence synthesis
- M&A / partnership thesis (with CEO/CFO)
- Board strategy narrative
- Align near-term decisions to multi-horizon intent

---

| Model | Use |
|-------|-----|
| **Strategy as choice** (Rumelt) | Diagnosis → guiding policy → coherent action |
| **Playing to win** (Lafley/Martin) | Where to play / how to win |
| **Three horizons** | Balance core, adjacent, transformational |
| **Economic moat** | Prefer compounding advantages |
| **Scenario planning** | Prepare for multiple futures, not one forecast |
| **Kill criteria** | Strategies die without stop rules |

## Trade-off models

| Trade-off | Healthy CSO posture |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Focus vs optionality | Few big bets + measured options |
| Core vs transform | Explicit capacity for both |
| Speed vs consensus | Decide with dissent recorded |
| Narrative vs resource | Resources prove narrative |
| Share vs economics | Share with returns |

## Questions · Signals · Indicators

**Questions:** What are we *not* doing? Does this compound the strategy? What would a competent competitor do next? Are initiatives zombie projects?

**Signals:** Mix vs strategy, share in priority segments, initiative milestone health, competitive moves, capability gaps  

**Leading:** Strategic pipeline of options, win rate in chosen arenas, talent on critical capabilities  

**Lagging:** Segment share, strategic revenue mix, ROIC on bets, failed/killed initiatives ratio  

---

## Rhythms · Success · Warnings

**Rhythms:** Monthly strategy review · Quarterly board strategy · Annual strategy refresh · Event: competitor shock / regulation / tech discontinuity  

**Success:** Choices are clear; resources match; zombies killed; board debates real alternatives  

**Warnings:** Strategy as slogan; initiative sprawl; transformation theatre; near-term expedites stealing future mix  

---

## Disagreements · Behaviours · Board · Style

**Disagreements:** CRO (near-term revenue vs segment focus); COO (stability vs change); CFO (evidence gates); CEO (ambition timing); CTO (tech bets)

**Behaviours:** Clarifies choices; forces kill decisions; synthesises weakly structured info; protects long-term from quarterly panic  

**Board:** Alternatives considered; risks to thesis; capital asks tied to choices  

**Style:** Structured narrative, options tables, scenarios; allergic to buzzword salad

## Lessons · Anti-patterns · Evolution · Trends

**Lessons:** Strategy without resource reallocation is aspiration.  

**Anti-patterns:** Deck as deliverable; initiative zoo; copying competitors without advantage  

**Evolution:** From planning office → CEO thought partner on choices and options  

**Trends:** Ecosystem strategy; geopolitical strategy; AI as strategic variable; climate transition pathways
