**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## Purpose of the role

The CCO owns **customer value realisation and relationship health** across the lifecycle. Exceptional CCOs make retention, expansion, and advocacy outcomes of delivered value — not of discounting or ticket deflection.

---

## Primary responsibilities

- Customer success / experience system design
- Retention, expansion, and advocacy programmes
- Voice of customer into product and operations
- Major account health and escalation governance
- Customer economics (with CRO/CFO)
- Experience standards and journey ownership

---

| Model | Use |

| Trade-off | Healthy CCO posture |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Save at all costs vs portfolio health | Economics + strategy |
| Standardisation vs high-touch | Segmented |
| NPS theatre vs operational fix | Fix root causes |
| Product backlog vs CS workarounds | Prefer product |

## Questions · Signals · Indicators

**Questions:** Did the customer achieve the outcome we sold? Who is silently failing? What is the save worth? What product debt creates CS load?

**Signals:** Health scores, product usage, support effort, executive sponsor engagement, renewal risk  

**Leading:** Time-to-value, adoption depth, effort score, early warning health  

**Lagging:** GRR/NRR, logo churn, CSAT/NPS, expansion rate, referenceability  

---

## Rhythms · Success · Warnings

**Rhythms:** Weekly risk book · Monthly experience review · Quarterly QBR programme · Event: major customer incident  

**Success:** Retention in band; strategic accounts stable; feedback changes roadmap; low heroics  

**Warnings:** Health scores ignored; CS as discount desk; QBR theatre; support volume masking product failure  

---

## Disagreements · Behaviours · Board · Style

**Disagreements:** CRO (who owns renewals); CFO (investment lag); COO/CTO (delivery quality); CPO (talent in CS)

**Behaviours:** Customer advocate with economic spine; escalates early; kills zombie accounts honestly  

**Board:** Retention quality; concentration; experience risk  

**Style:** Empathic but numeric; story + cohort evidence

## Lessons · Anti-patterns · Evolution · Trends

**Lessons:** You cannot CS your way out of a broken product.  

**Anti-patterns:** NPS as goal; save-at-all-costs; QBR slides without action  

**Evolution:** From support leader → enterprise customer outcomes owner  

**Trends:** Product-led success; AI support assist; outcome-based commercial models
